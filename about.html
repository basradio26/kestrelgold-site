About Kestrel Gold

The Legend Returns to Basingstoke

In 1998, a new sound took over the airwaves of North Hampshire. 107.6 Kestrel FM wasn’t just a radio station — it became the heartbeat of Basingstoke. From unforgettable breakfast shows to the local news that kept the community connected, Kestrel FM was the “truly local” voice people woke up to every morning.

For more than a decade, it defined the sound of our town, bringing listeners closer to the community and the music that shaped their lives. Even as frequencies changed and national networks took over, the memory of that iconic Kestrel spirit never faded.

The Revival — Powered by Basingstoke Radio

You asked for it, and we listened. Basingstoke Radio is proud to bring a local legend back to life.

Kestrel Gold is the official revival of the original Kestrel brand — a station dedicated to the heritage, the town, and the timeless hits that made Kestrel FM so special. We’re taking everything you loved about the “Great Kestrel Days” and bringing it into the digital age.

This isn’t just a relaunch. It’s a homecoming for listeners who remember when local radio truly felt like home.

The Soundtrack of Your Life

At Kestrel Gold, we don’t just play the hits — we play your memories.

  • The Songs You Love: Powerhouse anthems of the 70s and 80s, plus the feel‑good classics of the 90s.
  • The Stories You Remember: Reliving the golden era of North Hampshire broadcasting.
  • Pure Nostalgia: No filler, no clutter — just the greatest music ever made, delivered with that unmistakable Kestrel energy.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to honour the legacy of the original Kestrel FM by delivering a high‑quality, nostalgic listening experience tailored specifically for the people of Basingstoke.
